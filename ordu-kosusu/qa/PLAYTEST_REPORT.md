# Ordu Koşusu mechanics playtest report

Build: merge of ccr-2a6d9cbd-mtl4ix (e74e4d8). Scripts: `qa/play/t1..t10.js` (run `node qa/play/tN.js` after `node build.mjs`; `t7.js` = bot win-rate with trials, `t36.js` = event trace of a level, `LV=36 UP=0`). Screenshots: `qa/out/play_*.png`. No game code was edited.

## Findings (ordered by importance)

1. **[blocker/design] Weapon tier, rate/dmg upgrades and weapon crates are cancelled out by enemy/wall/boss HP scaling.**
   `main.js:armObjects` sets enemy `g.hp = sqrt(T.hp)*hpMul*(curDps()/S.count)*1.1`, wall/crate `hp = dps*frac*tf`, and `enterBoss` `hp = curDps()*(9+...)`. All scale with the player's current DPS, so a better gun/upgrade gives the same time-to-kill. Evidence (t36 L36): enemy hp/each 4.46 at upgrade 0, 16.0 at upgrade 10, 31.5 after a tier-1 weapon. Boss fight lasts 9-15 s whatever the army (t8). L36 bot win-rate: up0 0/8, up4 0/5, up10 0/5, up20 (~45k coins per upgrade, unreachable) 1/5 (t7). Only objects armed before a pickup benefit (arm distance 85). Upgrades are effectively cosmetic. Fix: scale enemy/wall/boss HP by level (and army size), not by weapon/upgrade multipliers.
2. **[major] Level 36 is a real spike (sim bot 0/8, same death point z=359 each trial).** Layout (t6): L36 has the highest threat count of L1-40 (6 enemy groups, 4 demon shooters, 6 saws, 3 walls = 15) against only 6 growth items (4 gates + 2 soldier crates); max group factor f~1.68, hpMul 1.43, speed 10 (fastest). Trace (`LV=36 node qa/play/t36.js`): army peaks at 39 (z=172); demon shooters (40 demons, ~11 bullets/s; "shot+12..19" in a single second) plus walls take it to 10-16; the 22-skeleton group (sized from the army at arm time) meets 10 soldiers and wipes them in ~0.5 s (melee: 1 of ours per tick, only 1/hp^0.35 of theirs; skeleton hp 2.0, demon 2.2). L31-35 and L37-40 are won 8/8, so it is layout variance, not the curve. Fixes: cap threat budget per level or reroll seeds with a solvability check (sim bot); guarantee minimum growth items; cap shooter groups; ease f/hpMul on the L>30 plateau.
3. **[major] Saws are far too lethal for big armies and a wide formation cannot dodge them (t2).** A saw kills every soldier it touches; a long column stays in the blade ~1.3 s. Deaths passing one saw (mean/max over 8 phases): n=20: 2.5/8 centre, 4.8/19 edge; n=60: 8.8/31 centre, 14/52 edge; n=150: 35/100 centre, 42/124 edge (83% of the army). Parking at the edge does not avoid it (blade reach ±4.1 vs army reaching ±4.35). Sim: L40 loses with 158 saw deaths (up4); bot L40 88-92 saw deaths/run, L30 34. Fix: cap kills per saw pass (hit cooldown), lower amp/speed, or leave a safe lane. (`main.js:updateObstacles` saws loop; `levels.js:genLevel` saw amp 3.4.)
4. **[major] Pause then Home during the win celebration loses the reward and level progress.** `finishWin` runs only after `endT > 2.4`; gear is clickable in state 'win' and Home calls `showMenu()`. t8: after boss death, Gear + Home leaves `save.level` 3 and coins 0. Fix: hide gear while state==='win', or bank reward/level immediately in `hitBoss`.
5. **[major] ROKETATAR (tier 4) is unreachable.** Weapon resets to 0 each level (`buildLevel`) and each level has at most 3 weapon crates (`genLevel`), so max tier is 3 (PLAZMA); sim always shows weapon<=3. The "5th tier becomes soldier crate" branch in `armObjects` is dead. Keep weapon across levels or add crates.
6. **[major] Stale-tier duplicate weapon crates.** Crates are armed 85 m ahead with `tier = S.weapon+1`; when two weapon crates are <~80 m apart both get the same tier and the second does nothing but still plays popup/sound (t4: L3 z179/z227 "weapon 2->2"; close pairs also L1,4,5,11,12,13,21). Lost progression plus misleading popup. Fix: decide tier in `blockDestroyed` (`S.weapon+1`), refresh label, skip popup when no upgrade.
7. **[major] Objects sized from the army at arm time (85 m ahead), not at arrival.** Enemy count, wall/crate HP, gate values use `S.count` ~3 segments earlier. Losses en route leave the army much smaller than the group sized for it (L36 skeleton group sized vs 56, met at 42 then 10). A gate "−N" can exceed arrival count (other gate is always good, so avoidable). Fix: size lazily at ~30 m.
8. **[major/balance] Melee enemies get ~2.4 s of fire and out-trade the player in melee.** Idle groups stand until dz<16 (`updateGroups`), bullet range ~28, closing ~15 m/s once charging. Per melee tick we lose 1 and they lose `1/hp^0.35`. t5 (weapon 0, army standing still): L31 skeletons vs 120 army, f=1.35: 25 left; vs 30: 9 left; L36 vs 5 army f=1.35: loses. Consider earlier engagement / softer exchange.
9. **[minor/major] Enemy shooters very lethal at scale**: `min(count*rate,14)` bullets/s, 1 kill per hit, aimed at first 12 soldiers (`updateEnemyFire`). 12-19 deaths in one second at L36.
10. **[minor] Boss slam is unavoidable** (`updateBoss`: kills `max(2, ceil(11% count))` every 1.55 s regardless of x). Fight length scales with level (HP uses `LV.L`): W4 boss takes 47% of a 60 army (L31) vs 12% at L1 (t8). Consider a telegraph/x-range.
11. **[minor] Unbroken crate** is destroyed by any touching soldier with a red "KAÇTI!" toast, no reward (matches code comment; fine). Crate HP label is small.
12. **[minor] Gates: ÷2 at n=1 is a no-op** (ceil(1/2)=1); 100/226 gates at n=1 contain it (t3). Otherwise values sane for n=1,5,50,500 (e.g. n=500: +485/+350, +460/−205; never both bad, never a wipe). Side is decided by army centre `squad.x<0` (exactly 0 goes to panel index 1 = screen-left); a 6-wide army straddling the middle post counts fully for one side (polish).
13. **[minor] Upgrade cost curve explodes**: `40*1.42^lvl` (lvl10 ~1.3k, 20 ~45k, 29 ~1M) vs income ~20+6L+0.6*count (~250-300/level). With #1, upgrades feel pointless; max levels 40/30/30 unreachable.
14. **[polish] First-run tutorial text overlays the army** (`qa/out/play_army1000.png`).

## Verified OK
- Steering: right drag moves army to screen-right (ndc +0.66 after 100 px); clamp ±4.35 for 1 soldier, ±3.2 centre for 6+ columns; 6 abreast, rows behind, 150 visible cap, nothing outside road (t1).
- Shooting: round-robin by construction, no-target shots go straight (vx=0), aim cone 0.35; damage = dps / visible shots, linear in count.
- Weapon crate: tier up + soldier model swap + popup work (t4 L1-L4).
- Walls: HP ∝ army, crash costs >=12 soldiers, avoidable. Saws kill only on contact. Enemy bullets kill only when overlapping a soldier; 1-soldier army dodges melee group (t9).
- Bosses at L1/11/21/31 walk ~7-8 s, attack, HP bar, win panel; "Yeni bölüm açıldı" at L10, level 11 = ÇÖL after Next; level/coins/upgrades persist after reload (t8). 1 or 5 soldiers vs boss lose; 1000 win (0.07 ms/step).
- Retry after lose returns to menu on same level, run coins banked. Count 0 mid-fight gives a single lose(), no errors. Gear pause freezes the game (t10). Rapid taps / swipe frenzy: no page errors. Screenshots: play_army1000, play_boss31_big, play_pre_pause_fight, play_paused, play_gate_early.
